## Free components

The Desyne component library (every free component, block and theme in these docs, and
the [source repository](https://github.com/doqett/desyne)) is released under the
[MIT License](https://github.com/doqett/desyne/blob/main/LICENSE). Use it in any
project, personal or commercial. Once installed, the source is yours: modify it, ship it
in products you sell, keep it forever. Keep the copyright notice if you redistribute the
library itself.

## Pro blocks and templates

Pro blocks and templates are **not** covered by the MIT License. They are licensed
separately under the Desyne Pro license, and their source is not in the public
repository. A Pro or Team license lets you:

- Use Pro blocks and templates in unlimited client and commercial projects.
- Modify everything, and ship it inside apps and sites you sell.
- Keep using everything you downloaded, forever.

You can't:

- Resell or redistribute Pro blocks or templates as a kit, theme or template.
- Publish Pro source in a public repository.
- Share a personal (Pro) license with your team. That's what Team is for.

A Pro license covers one developer; Team covers up to ten. See
[Licensing & seats](/docs/pro/licensing) for keys and team management.

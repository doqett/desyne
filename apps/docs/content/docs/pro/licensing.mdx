## How a purchase works

1. Choose **Pro** or **Team** on the pricing page. Checkout opens in a secure Paddle
   window; Paddle is our merchant of record and handles payment, sales tax and VAT, and
   your receipt. If you're signed in, your email is prefilled and the purchase is linked
   to your account straight away.
2. After payment you land on your **Account** page. It refreshes for a few seconds
   while the order is confirmed, then shows your license.
3. Each license has a key: `dsp_` followed by 32 characters.

Not signed in when you bought? Sign in later with the same email address. Licenses are
attached to accounts by verified email, so the purchase appears automatically.

## Signing in

The Pro site uses passwordless sign-in:

- **Email link.** Enter your email and click the link we send.
- **GitHub**, where enabled. Accounts with the same verified email are linked.

Signed in with an active license, you can see every block's source and download
templates in the browser without entering a key.

## Your key

The Account page shows your key masked, with buttons to reveal and copy it, and
ready-to-copy snippets for `components.json`, `.env.local` and a first install
command.

| Where the key is used | How |
| --- | --- |
| shadcn CLI | `Authorization: Bearer ${DESYNE_PRO_LICENSE}` header in `components.json` ([setup](/docs/pro/blocks)) |
| A browser without signing in | **Unlock Pro** dialog → "use a license key". Stored in a secure, HTTP-only cookie for that browser |
| CI | A `DESYNE_PRO_LICENSE` secret, only if the pipeline runs the CLI |

Treat the key like a password: keep it in `.env` files and CI secrets, never in code you
commit.

### Regenerating

If a key leaks, press **Regenerate key** on the Account page. The old key stops working
immediately, everywhere: update `.env.local` files and CI secrets with the new one.
Teammates' personal keys on a Team license keep working.

## Team seats

A Team license has **10 seats**, and the owner uses one.

- **Invite** a teammate by email from the Account page. They get access as soon as they
  sign in with that (verified) email address.
- Each member gets a **personal key**, shown on their own Account page, for their CLI
  setup. They don't need the owner's key.
- **Remove** a member to free the seat. Their personal key stops working immediately.
- When all seats are in use, remove someone before inviting another person.

Pro licenses are for one developer and don't have seats; upgrade to Team to share
access.

## Refunds

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of purchase. Refunds are handled by
Paddle, our merchant of record: request one at [paddle.net](https://paddle.net) or
email support@desyne.dev. A full refund deactivates the license and every member key
on it, and the licence to use Pro code obtained under it ends; the account page then
shows the license as inactive. A partial refund leaves the license active. See the
[Refund Policy](https://pro.desyne.dev/refunds).
